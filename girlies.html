THE GIRLIES.

Watch the film ↓All projects ↗

The story

A GIRLS' NIGHT, WITH A TWIST.

It's about a girl that gets broken up with, so she contacts the Girlies — a group chat of her guy friends — to help her. She convinces them to do a girls' night with her to make her feel better. After some disagreement, they decide to do it.

MY CONTRIBUTION

As director of photography, I focused on the framing of the actors and how they would use body language in frame.

As co-editor, I assembled the timeline in Adobe Premiere Pro: cutting material before and after the actual content and placing it chronologically in the timeline.
