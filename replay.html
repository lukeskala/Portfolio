Case study 01 / Live sports production

THE TEN-SECOND DECISION.

G-MAC Women's Soccer Championship / Penalty shootout / Solo replay operation

Watch on FloCollege ↗All projects ↗

The situation

NO REDOS. NO TIME TO WASTE.

I worked replay for a penalty shootout in a playoff game. There were a lot of replays in quick succession, so I had to stay very focused throughout.

As the solo replay operator, my job was to watch the game from every angle. As soon as something replayable happened, I was on the clock to find the best angle, save it, and get it on screen live.

THE CHALLENGE

The hardest part was finding which angle was best and getting the replay ready within about 10 seconds.

WHAT I LEARNED

“You can really only watch a game from 1 camera so I got a lot of experience finding the best angle quickly.”

This sequence pushed me to make fast choices about which camera angle would show the moment most clearly.
